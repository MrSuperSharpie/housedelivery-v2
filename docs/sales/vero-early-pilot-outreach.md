## 6. What Vero is / is not

**Vero Permit IS:**

- A way to keep inspection records, photos, notes, videos, deficiencies, reports,
  field notes, and project history organized in one secure project file (the Vero Vault).
- A shared, clearer record for builders, inspectors, qualified professionals, project
  teams, owners, and authorities.
- Support for better-documented inspection files and smoother handover.

**Vero Permit is NOT:**

- A permit-approval platform.
- A replacement for the city, AHJ, building official, architect, engineer, registered
  professional, code consultant, Certified Professional, or qualified reviewer.
- A compliance, code, or legal decision-maker.
- A guarantee of approval or acceptance.

The city or proper authority always retains final say.

## 7. Objection handling

**"We already take photos and notes."**
Great — Vero just keeps them organized in one project file instead of spread across
phones, texts, and email, so nothing gets lost and handover is clean.

**"Does this replace my inspector or the city?"**
No. Your inspector does the inspection and the city or authority decides. Vero only
organizes the records around that work.

**"Is this a compliance or code tool?"**
No. Vero doesn't make compliance or code decisions and doesn't certify anything. It
keeps the documentation organized so the people who do make those decisions have a
clear record.

**"We're busy — is this a big change?"**
No rebuild of how you work. Photos and notes get captured the way you already do them;
Vero just puts them in one organized place.

**"What does it cost / what's the commitment?"**
For pilots we keep it simple and low-commitment — the goal is to see if it genuinely
saves you time on a real project before anyone commits to more.

**"What about our data?"**
Records live in a secure project file. Your findings and judgment stay yours.

## 8. Ideal first prospects

- Small-to-mid BC builders running 1–5 active projects who already feel the pain of
  scattered records.
- Independent private inspectors and qualified professionals who handle their own
  post-inspection paperwork.
- Builders doing single-family or small multi-unit work who hand records to owners or
  authorities and want a cleaner package.
- Teams who have recently lost or struggled to find inspection documentation.
- People in Edgar's existing network who will give honest feedback.

## 9. Phrases NOT to use (warning list)

- "Vero approves permits" / "permit approval"
- "Vero certifies compliance" / "code-certified" / "compliance certified"
- "Vero replaces the city" / "AHJ accepted" / "city-approved"
- "Vero guarantees approval"
- "Vero cuts permit wait times" / "faster permits"
- "Approved by Vero" / "certified by Vero"
- Anything implying Vero makes legal, code, or compliance decisions

Stay with: *organize inspection records, create clearer documentation, preserve project
history in the Vero Vault, the city or proper authority retains final say.*
