# Vero Permit — 7-Minute Demo Script

> Goal: show a builder or inspector that Vero keeps inspection records organized in one
> secure project file. Calm and practical. No approval, compliance, or code claims.
> The city or proper authority always retains final say.

**Before you start:** have one real (or realistic) project file open so you're showing
the product, not slides.

---

## 0:00–0:30 — Positioning (30 seconds)

"Here's the one-liner: **inspections, finally organized.**

Vero Permit keeps inspection records, photos, notes, deficiencies, reports, and project
history in one secure project file. It doesn't replace your inspector or the city — it
just keeps the record clean so nothing gets lost and handover is easy. Let me show you
on a real project."

---

## 0:30–1:15 — Builder pain question (~45 seconds)

Ask, then listen:

- "When you need an inspection photo or report from three months ago, where do you
  look right now?"
- "How much of it lives in texts, phones, or email threads?"

Reflect it back: "Right — so the information exists, it's just scattered. That's
exactly the gap Vero closes."

---

## 1:15–2:15 — Inspection request overview (~1 minute)

Show how a builder requests an inspection and what an inspector sees.

- Walk through creating/dispatching a request.
- Point out that the request carries the project context, so the record stays attached
  to the right project from the start.
- Keep it light: "The point here isn't the scheduling — it's that everything from this
  request flows into one organized file."

---

## 2:15–3:15 — Inspector workflow overview (~1 minute)

Switch to the inspector's view.

- Show how the inspector works through their inspection on site.
- Emphasize: "The inspector's findings and professional judgment stay theirs. Vero
  isn't deciding anything — it's capturing what they document, cleanly."

---

## 3:15–4:15 — Photos, notes, and evidence (~1 minute)

This is the core value. Show evidence capture.

- Add/point to photos, notes, deficiencies, and video evidence on an item.
- "Everything is captured against the right stage and item, so later you're not guessing
  which photo went with which part of the inspection."

---

## 4:15–5:00 — Field Note Record (~45 seconds)

Open a Field Note Record.

- "A field note used to be a raw text blob. Now it opens as a clean, printable Field
  Note Record with the project, address, inspector, stage, and the note itself — saveable
  as a PDF."
- "The original captured file is still preserved underneath as the audit artifact —
  we just present it properly."

---

## 5:00–6:00 — Report and the Vero Vault (~1 minute)

Show the assembled inspection report / package and the Vault.

- "All of this rolls up into an inspection report and a secure project file in the Vero
  Vault — photos, notes, deficiencies, reports, and history in one place."
- "When it's time to hand something to an owner or an authority, you're handing over a
  clean, organized package instead of a scramble."
- Honest framing: "This is your record. It's not an official submission until the
  authority receives it, and the authority still decides."

---

## 6:00–6:30 — Admin coverage diagnostic (internal confidence only) (~30 seconds)

> Use this **only** if it strengthens credibility with a hands-on prospect. It is an
> internal tool, **not** a buyer-facing compliance claim.

- "On our side, we have an internal diagnostic that shows where our checklist coverage
  is strong and where it's still maturing. We use it to be honest with ourselves about
  what's ready."
- Do **not** present it as a compliance score or an approval signal. It's an internal
  operations view.

---

## 6:30–7:00 — Pilot ask + close (~30 seconds)

"Here's what I'd suggest: pick one real project, and let's run it through Vero together
so you can feel whether it actually saves you time. Low commitment, real project, honest
feedback.

Would you be open to starting a pilot on your next inspection?"

Then stop talking and let them answer.

---

## Quick reference — say / don't say

**Say:** organize inspection records · one secure project file · clearer documentation ·
preserve project history in the Vero Vault · the authority retains final say.

**Don't say:** approves permits · certifies compliance · replaces the city · guarantees
approval · faster/cuts permit wait times · approved/certified by Vero.
