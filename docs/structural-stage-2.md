# Structural — Stage 2: Footings & Foundation / First Field Inspection

## S02-01 — Site Condition, Layout, and Inspection Readiness

**Purpose**  
Confirm that the site is ready for a meaningful foundation inspection and that the inspector is reviewing the correct stage at the correct time.

### What to Check
- Excavation or footing area is accessible for inspection  
- Site condition allows meaningful observation of the work  
- Work has not advanced beyond the intended inspection stage  
- The inspected area matches the approved layout and scope  

### Pass When
- Site is accessible and safe for inspection  
- Area is clearly identifiable  
- Work remains at the correct hold point  

### Fail When
- Site is not reasonably inspectable  
- Wrong area or stage is presented  
- Work has advanced beyond inspection hold point  

### Pending When
- Access or timing temporarily prevents reliable inspection  

### Inspector Notes Guidance
Record:
- Site condition  
- Accessibility  
- Whether correct hold point was presented  
- Any limitations  

### Required Evidence
- Inspector-captured site photos showing inspection readiness  

### Optional Evidence
- Site overview video  
- Builder clarification notes  
- Layout or staging photos  

### AHJ / Jurisdiction Notes
Capture any authority-specific hold-point or inspection timing requirements.
