# Windows & Patio Doors Look Book — source and specification notes

The new chapter gives every custom home four Premium and four Signature design boards (144 home-labelled boards across 18 homes). Each board shows a full room visualization plus window style, patio door, glazing, door profile and operating-hardware references. Premium directions show double-glazed windows; Signature directions show triple-glazed windows. Patio-door glass build-up is selected with the actual system and is not assumed to match the window tier. The collection covers casement, awning, fixed/picture and tilt-turn windows, plus conventional and large-format sliding, lift-and-slide, folding, and pocketing patio systems. Salt Spring has one shared building-wide glazing selection for both residences. Frame colour is coordinated with the exterior chosen separately for each home; the architectural window locations and wall openings are not changed by choosing a finish direction.

## Supplier material supplied by House Delivery

The published boards do not display supplier names, model numbers or watermarks. Their technical cutaways are cropped from the supplied catalogs, while the room scenes are representative visualizations. A board may combine reference details from different catalog systems to describe a design direction; it must never be read as a certified, purchasable combined assembly. The hardware tile is explicitly a **style reference** unless its final compatibility with the selected door system is verified. The source crops used by the renderer are documented below for internal review:

| Component crop | Catalog reference |
| --- | --- |
| Double-glazed casement, classic slider, folding door and sliding pull | DERCHI window/sliding/folding spreads (catalog pages 19, 37–40, 47–48) |
| Double-glazed tilt-turn, triple-glazed window and lift-slide door section | LEDOW C75, G91 and G152 product spreads (catalog pages 14, 33 and 21) |
| Folding handle style and dark architectural pull style | AMJ 80T bifold and 155T sliding-door product photographs (catalog pages 37–38 and 33–34) |

The pocketing option intentionally leaves the door-profile and hardware tiles as project-specific requirements because the supplied pocket-door illustration is for an interior partition. A qualifying weather-rated exterior system, pocket construction and tested reports must be selected for the site.

The [AMJ CSA report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tGq6SXdDgMRKG21XGDdCfAb3vvnagqUt/view) covers the 88T tilt-turn window and 155T sliding door at identified tested configurations and sizes. The 154T catalog shows a triple-glazed option; the catalog's maximum size is not the same as a certified tested size.
- [DERCHI catalog](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PAYuQNlUAoP-7KJpbsEHJpBEFKA6HQ8T/view) illustrates fixed/casement windows, multi-track sliding doors and folding doors. Its [CSA certificate](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1i-LhBcRGRftyUDxwg0h45NBEgA9YKvS4/view) identifies the 104/128 window, 80 folding-door and P103 swing-door families. It does not establish certification for every sliding-door product in the catalog. The eight architectural scenes reused in the home-labelled boards are representative visualizations of the design directions, not photographs or certifications of specific supplier models.
- DERCHI catalog pages 047–048 show a useful *presentation structure* for folding doors: opening action, glass build-up, frame and thermal break, maximum panel size, hardware, plus air, water, wind and thermal figures. The WZ-78 and WZ-80 examples both list **double-glazed** door build-ups (5+27A+5 mm and 5+20A+5 mm respectively). Their published figures belong only to those catalog models and must not be attached to a different triple-glazed door design, treated as whole-assembly Canadian compliance for all sizes, or promoted as an inclusion before the exact product is selected and its reports verified. The customer-facing Look Book therefore shows a concise specification guide and leaves numeric ratings to the project product schedule.
- [LEDOW aluminum collection](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oXuW0SXYhogFAZxLwSAs-NAskbpv6LgW/view) shows C75 tilt-turn windows, G152/G185/G165/G120 lift-and-slide systems and G88 outward folding systems. Its pocket-door spread illustrates an interior partition; it is **not** evidence of a weather-rated exterior pocketing assembly. The Look Book presents pocketing patio glass as a design direction for a separately selected exterior-rated assembly.
- [Ximulong S96 report](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jeufffzNYtl90kbzTqtlI10584Kju1BS?usp=drive_link) concerns a window-frame component. It is not a complete-window or patio-door performance report.

## Canadian project schedule

The selected design direction becomes a project-specific fenestration schedule before ordering. Record the exact manufacturer, model, operating type, rough opening and panel sizes, glazing build-up, thermally broken frame, finish, hardware, insect-screen provision and accessibility/threshold detail. Obtain the applicable complete-assembly NAFS/CSA A440S1 classification and whole-unit thermal documentation, then confirm the site's wind and rain exposure, energy target, safety glazing, egress, fall protection, condensation control, flashing and drainage. For pocketing systems, resolve the concealed pocket, structure, thermal continuity, water management and service access with the selected exterior-rated manufacturer.

Natural Resources Canada's [current ENERGY STAR Canada fenestration specification](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/windows-doors-skylights-energy-star-canada-technical-specification) sets a national voluntary energy benchmark and explicitly says that ENERGY STAR qualification does not replace other code or regulatory requirements. The final permitted specification follows the applicable provincial/territorial code and local authority for the site.
