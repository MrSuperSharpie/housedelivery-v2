# Windows & Patio Doors Look Book — source and specification notes

The chapter gives every custom home four Premium and four Signature design boards (144 home-labelled boards across 18 homes). The corrected boards use crops from the supplied supplier catalogs: product lifestyle imagery, actual profile sections, manufacturer operating diagrams where provided, and hardware photos where provided. Each identifies the supplier and model, catalog glass build-up or explicitly unverified glass configuration, hardware name and source page. No section, mechanism, handle or glass-stack diagram is invented. Salt Spring has one shared building-wide glazing selection for both residences. The named model is a sourced visual and product reference; the final inclusion and Canadian project specification are still confirmed during project review.

## Supplier material supplied by House Delivery

- [AMJ product catalog](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tLDoTomUpSVRqbe1aVRmvNy1yHPRCQGr/view) shows operable windows, 100T/154T/155T sliding doors, 68T/80T bifolds and 150T glass-wall systems. The [AMJ CSA report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tGq6SXdDgMRKG21XGDdCfAb3vvnagqUt/view) covers the 88T tilt-turn window and 155T sliding door at identified tested configurations and sizes. The 154T catalog shows a triple-glazed option; the catalog's maximum size is not the same as a certified tested size.
- [DERCHI catalog](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PAYuQNlUAoP-7KJpbsEHJpBEFKA6HQ8T/view) illustrates fixed/casement windows, multi-track sliding doors and folding doors. Its [CSA certificate](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1i-LhBcRGRftyUDxwg0h45NBEgA9YKvS4/view) identifies the 104/128 window, 80 folding-door and P103 swing-door families. It does not establish certification for every sliding-door product in the catalog.
- DERCHI catalog pages 047–048 show a useful *presentation structure* for folding doors: opening action, glass build-up, frame and thermal break, maximum panel size, hardware, plus air, water, wind and thermal figures. The WZ-78 and WZ-80 examples both list **double-glazed** door build-ups (5+27A+5 mm and 5+20A+5 mm respectively). Their published figures belong only to those catalog models and must not be attached to a different triple-glazed door design, treated as whole-assembly Canadian compliance for all sizes, or promoted as an inclusion before the exact product is selected and its reports verified. The customer-facing Look Book therefore shows a concise specification guide and leaves numeric ratings to the project product schedule.
- [LEDOW aluminum collection](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oXuW0SXYhogFAZxLwSAs-NAskbpv6LgW/view) shows C75 tilt-turn windows, G152/G185/G165/G120 lift-and-slide systems and G88 outward folding systems. Its pocket-door spread illustrates an interior partition; it is **not** evidence of a weather-rated exterior pocketing assembly. The corrected Look Book omits exterior pocketing until a real weather-rated supplier model and documentation are supplied.
- [Ximulong S96 report](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jeufffzNYtl90kbzTqtlI10584Kju1BS?usp=drive_link) concerns a window-frame component. It is not a complete-window or patio-door performance report.

## Canadian project schedule

The selected design direction becomes a project-specific fenestration schedule before ordering. Record the exact manufacturer, model, operating type, rough opening and panel sizes, glazing build-up, thermally broken frame, finish, hardware, insect-screen provision and accessibility/threshold detail. Obtain the applicable complete-assembly NAFS/CSA A440S1 classification and whole-unit thermal documentation, then confirm the site's wind and rain exposure, energy target, safety glazing, egress, fall protection, condensation control, flashing and drainage. The supplied catalogs show double-glazed standard build-ups for several selected models and an optional triple-glazed build-up only where explicitly stated; a blanket triple-pane claim must not be inferred from the appearance of a board.

Natural Resources Canada's [current ENERGY STAR Canada fenestration specification](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/energy-efficiency/windows-doors-skylights-energy-star-canada-technical-specification) sets a national voluntary energy benchmark and explicitly says that ENERGY STAR qualification does not replace other code or regulatory requirements. The final permitted specification follows the applicable provincial/territorial code and local authority for the site.
