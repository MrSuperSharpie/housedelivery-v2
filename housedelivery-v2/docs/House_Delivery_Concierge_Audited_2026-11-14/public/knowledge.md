# Customer-safe knowledge guide

Edition 2026-11-14.qa1. The JSON is the factual integration candidate. This guide explains how to use it; it is not a runtime prompt or FAQ index.

## Positioning and customer needs

House Delivery is an Indigenous-owned Canadian housing company serving repeatable development and community programs alongside individual homes and ADUs. Start with the visitor’s purpose, answer their question, then help them compare a suitable design or define a useful project next step. Quality, durability, serviceability and lasting value guide the discussion; no numerical savings or certification is implied. ## How to recommend

Use housing family and unit basis first, then bedrooms, bathrooms and hard limits. Rank compatible records by size distance; use a 10% initial tolerance for “around” as a recommendation policy. An outside-range alternative must be labelled as such. Never turn a hard maximum into an approximate target. A compact search includes both compact homes and the Micro/Mini ADUs. Unknown or conflicting areas cannot win a numeric match.

Examples: Aurora is the closest four-bedroom match around 2,500 sq. ft.; Canmore/Cascade around 3,500; Saturna/Maplewood for five bedrooms around 3,500. There is no published three-bedroom detached match around 1,800 sq. ft. Keep that as a custom brief. These are derived comparisons from the model records.

## Pricing and inclusions

The current observed Salt Spring controls display Premium $250 and Signature $300 per sq. ft. The approved public statement identifies CAD package planning references. This does not validate a price for every model or a finished Canadian home. The corrected registry no longer assigns that rate rule to every architectural model. [S-M-SALT-SPRING](https://www.housedelivery.ca/homes/salt-spring); S-APPROVED-PRICE (approved public statement, version 2026-11-13.2)

A requested calculation can be answered directly: 2,486 × 250 = 621,500, using the visitor’s stated inputs. Label the result as arithmetic, not an Aurora offer. No Essential, universal ADU or turnkey rate is verified. No mixed-tier Salt Spring total can be calculated without confirmed separate residence areas.

The package consists of engineered components and selected materials. The written quotation defines contents, finishes, freight and applicable import costs. Site preparation, foundations, services, local professional work/permits, Canadian assembly/completion, appliances and sales taxes are separately considered unless expressly included. [S-CUSTOM](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/custom-homes); [S-LANEWAY](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/laneway-carriage-homes)

Essential is the durable baseline, Premium adds refinement and Signature adds selected feature finishes. Architectural custom homes list Premium/Signature. The compact/catalogue family lists Essential/Premium; the precise catalogue default needs confirmation because the homepage describes Premium as the starting point. ## Design, delivery and project readiness

The Studio coordinates the chosen home’s finishes into a Look Book. Its pictured materials and named directions are design intent; the final specification and quotation govern products, quantities and price. Structural, layout and exterior changes require project review. Do not infer product composition from palette names. [S-INCLUSIONS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/inclusions); [S-M-SOLACE](https://www.housedelivery.ca/homes/solace); [S-PROCESS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/how-it-works)

Systems may include domestic timber or internationally manufactured light-gauge steel, with Canadian site work, assembly and completion by qualified construction teams. An exact country is project-specific; a confidential factory identity is not a public product fact. A benign origin or warranty question deserves a direct answer. [S-CUSTOM](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/custom-homes); [S-LANEWAY](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/laneway-carriage-homes); [S-WARRANTY](https://www.housedelivery.ca/warranty)

Start with existing site information and needs, choose the home mix and design direction, establish scope/readiness and authorization, then coordinate professional work, approvals, manufacture, site preparation, logistics, assembly and handover. Some work can overlap. Dates, approvals, occupancy and capacity are project-specific. [S-PROCESS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/how-it-works)

The preliminary property check is a starting point using available government information, not a survey or approval. CMHC catalogue designs require professional adaptation and applicable approvals; featuring them does not imply CMHC endorsement. [S-LANEWAY](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/laneway-carriage-homes); [S-TERMS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/cmhc-catalogue-terms); [S-CMHC-OFFICIAL](https://assets.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/sites/housing%20catalog/resources/housing-design-catalogue-user-terms-and-conditions.pdf?rev=8aa4ec1f-26db-4c8f-b542-fc502732904d)

## Conversation and confidentiality rules

Answer first, normally in two to four short sentences. Offer one or two suitable designs with a reason; ask at most one useful follow-up, and sometimes none. Remember corrections, selected models, the visitor’s site and their decision not to give contact details. Link directly to a known model or next step. Do not repeatedly recite project-review caveats after simple bedroom or size answers.

Only the selected JSON is eligible factual input. Evaluation and maintainer material remain offline. The assistant has no authority to access private supplier, customer, procurement or correspondence records; no runtime browsing or arbitrary file tools. A contact request uses the existing separate consented form and is not “sent” until success is observed. Actual processing and retention notices must match the implementation.
