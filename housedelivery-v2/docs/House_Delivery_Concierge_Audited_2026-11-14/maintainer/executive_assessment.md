# Executive assessment

**Decision: use the completed research, with the corrections in this edition. Do not restart it. The corrected knowledge is suitable for Codex integration; the running Concierge is not yet launch-certified.**

The original work is a strong source inventory with useful product comparisons and sound commercial boundaries. Its 31-model catalogue and most customer facts hold up against the retained primary evidence. The largest weaknesses are in how that knowledge becomes a conversation, how broadly pricing is applied, and what the reported tests actually prove.

## Materials and review scope

All core materials were available: the knowledge JSON and Markdown, source register, 150 Q&As, 25 three-turn conversations, 15 security cases, implementation brief, gap report, matching helper and validation files. The previously inspected 255-fact approved snapshot and Concierge source files were also available. No missing document required restarting research.

What was not available as verified evidence was a current preview execution report, current deployment-to-commit mapping, tested lead-delivery outcome, full interactive Studio/Look Book output, or a confirmed AI-specific processing/retention notice. Those are implementation checks, not reasons to repeat product or supplier research.

## Facts that remain qualified

The BC Duplex drawing visibly lists three storeys while its model page says two. The Sixplex drawing matrix visibly gives 573.9 m², about 6,177 sq. ft., versus the page’s 6,216 sq. ft. These are contained at the affected fields; the designs can still be discussed for their supported unit mixes. Essential/Premium availability versus a Premium starting point is an unresolved default, not necessarily a contradiction.

Unknown compact areas, precise quoted area bases, project-specific construction totals and exact component origins remain unknown. They do not prevent useful model comparisons or project inquiries. There is no need to wait for another sourcing exercise to launch a factual, properly scoped Concierge.
