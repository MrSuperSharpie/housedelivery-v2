# The 50 launch-priority customer questions

Edition 2026-11-14.qa1. Answers are reference standards, not scripts. One optional follow-up is shown; use it only when useful and not already answered. Source links identify evidence, not content to fetch during a customer chat.

## Individual homes

### P01 · Can you help me with just one home?

Yes. We work with individual homeowners as well as developers, municipalities and community housing programs. We can start with your property, a design you like or the requirements for your home.

**Optional next question:** Where are you hoping to build?

**Variants:** Is one house too small a project for you? / We are a family, not a developer. Can we work with you?

**Evidence:** published. [S-CUSTOM](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/custom-homes); [S-PLANNER](https://www.housedelivery.ca/plan-a-housing-project).

**Acceptance:** Answer yes directly; do not diminish the national development business.

Original references: Q001.

**Evidence:** published. [S-PROCESS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/how-it-works); [S-CUSTOM](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/custom-homes).

**Acceptance:** Preserve existing builder/GC state; no unquoted turnkey promise.

Original references: Q094, Q095, Q126.

### P41 · Who helps if there is a warranty issue?

House Delivery helps identify the component, locate the applicable warranty documents and coordinate the next step. Product coverage and local installation or construction responsibilities follow the relevant project agreements.

**Optional next question:** Is this a product question or an installation issue?

**Variants:** How do manufacturer warranties work? / Will you help me find the right support contact?

**Evidence:** published. [S-WARRANTY](https://www.housedelivery.ca/warranty).

**Acceptance:** Answer benign manufacturer wording; no universal warranty duration or guaranteed replacement.

## Property and timing

### P42 · How do I find out whether a home will fit my property?

The preliminary property check is a useful starting point using available government information. A proper fit review considers the proposed layout, site dimensions, access, services and local requirements; the check is not a survey or an approval.

**Optional next question:** Which municipality is the property in?

**Variants:** My lot is narrow. What fits? / Can the free property check confirm my zoning?

**Evidence:** published. [S-LANEWAY](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/laneway-carriage-homes); [S-CUSTOM](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/custom-homes).

**Acceptance:** Use known location, route to existing check; do not claim to perform lookup in chat.

Original references: Q029, Q106, Q107, Q116.

### P43 · Do CMHC catalogue plans mean the home is already approved?

No. They are reference designs for project-specific adaptation by qualified professionals, with the applicable local approvals still required. Their use does not mean CMHC endorses or certifies House Delivery.

**Optional next question:** Which design and location are involved?

**Variants:** Can I submit the PDF and start building? / Does pre-reviewed mean no building permit?

**Evidence:** published. [S-TERMS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/cmhc-catalogue-terms); [S-CMHC-OFFICIAL](https://assets.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/sites/housing%20catalog/resources/housing-design-catalogue-user-terms-and-conditions.pdf?rev=8aa4ec1f-26db-4c8f-b542-fc502732904d).

**Acceptance:** No automatic permit, manufacturer certification or CMHC endorsement.

Original references: Q108, Q109, Q110.

### P44 · How quickly could the home be ready?

Timing depends on the design, approvals, site readiness, manufacturing, delivery and Canadian construction. Some work can run in parallel, and the team can establish a project schedule once those steps are defined.

**Optional next question:** What stage has your project reached?

**Variants:** Can we move in in four months? / Can manufacturing happen while foundations are prepared?

**Evidence:** project_specific. [S-PROCESS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/how-it-works); [S-HOME](https://www.housedelivery.ca).

**Acceptance:** No universal date; for explicit four-month commitment, say it cannot be confirmed from current information.

Original references: Q112, Q113, Q096.

### P45 · Can you discuss a phased program of 50 or 500 homes?

Yes. We can discuss the housing mix, repeatable designs and phased delivery as one program, with each phase planned around the sites and construction requirements. Production allocations and dates would be agreed through the project review.

**Optional next question:** What would the first phase include?

### P46 · Which fourplex could provide four three-bedroom homes?

BC Fourplex 2 is a three-storey reference design with four three-bedroom, two-bathroom homes. Its published 5,985 sq. ft. is the combined building area, and the design needs site-specific review.

**Optional next question:** Where is the proposed site?

**Variants:** We need four family rentals, all three beds. / Is the fourplex area per unit?

Original references: Q042, Q127, Q128.

### P47 · We are a municipality or institution. What is the next step?

Start with the housing need, location, intended households and what is already known about the site and delivery partners. The housing-project planner can organize that for discussion, whether your organization is the buyer, landowner, facilitator or operator.

**Optional next question:** What role would your organization have in the project?

Original references: Q124, Q125, Q130, Q135.

## First Nations and community housing

### P48 · Can our Nation lead the design and housing mix?

Yes. House Delivery is Indigenous-owned, and the community pathway starts with the Nation’s housing needs, land and priorities. The design direction is developed with the community and its chosen partners, including cultural expression, privacy, gathering and multigenerational living.

**Optional next question:** Which household needs should guide the first phase?

**Variants:** Do we have to choose a fixed community package? / Can the housing reflect our Nation’s priorities and culture?

**Evidence:** published. [S-CUSTOM](https://www.housedelivery.ca/build-your-home/custom-homes); [S-NATIONS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/first-nations-inspired); [S-PROCESS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/how-it-works).

**Acceptance:** Nation-led planning, no stereotyped design assumptions or invented commitments.

Original references: Q136, Q137, Q138, Q144.

### P49 · Can we keep employment and skills in the community?

Local participation and assembly training can be included where appropriate, with roles and resources developed around the project. We would start with the workforce and construction capacity the community wants to involve.

**Optional next question:** Do you have local construction or training partners in mind?

**Variants:** Our Nation wants local jobs and training. / How can community members take part in assembly?

**Evidence:** published. [S-NATIONS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/first-nations-inspired); [S-FNPLANNER](https://www.housedelivery.ca/first-nations-project-planner).

**Acceptance:** No job count, ratio, funding or guaranteed employment promise.

Original references: Q139, Q141, Q150.

### P50 · Can we start planning before funding is approved?

Yes. The First Nations planner can organize the housing needs, quantities, sites and project readiness using what is already known. It does not establish funding eligibility or approval, and Indigenous ownership alone does not guarantee a grant.

**Optional next question:** What is already known about the first phase?

**Variants:** We need 20 homes but do not have funding yet. / Does using an Indigenous-owned housing company automatically qualify us?

**Evidence:** published. [S-FNPLANNER](https://www.housedelivery.ca/first-nations-project-planner); [S-NATIONS](https://www.housedelivery.ca/first-nations-inspired).

**Acceptance:** Support early planning, preserve actual funding status, no funding guarantee.

Original references: Q146, Q147, Q150.
