SiteLine Platform Overview
SiteLine is a premium, governed marketplace built to sell two outcomes: Risk Mitigation and Velocity.
The platform is designed to reduce permitting friction, shorten inspection delays, improve compliance confidence, and generate a defensible professional record for builders, inspectors, administrators, and final reviewing authorities.
SiteLine has five main sections:
•	Admin — where SiteLine management reviews documents, approves or denies applications, oversees governed dispatch exceptions, manages compliance, controls payouts and disputes, and administers records and archives 
•	Builder 
•	Inspector / Certified Professional 
•	Authority Reviewer / Final Authority — the final reviewing and approval body for the submitted package 
•	Vault — SiteLine’s secure long-term archive for completed inspection records, evidence, and packaged submissions 
 
1. Builder Onboarding
A Builder creates an account and uploads all required company, permit, insurance, and compliance documentation needed to access the SiteLine platform.
The SiteLine Admin team reviews the Builder’s submission and either approves, denies, or requests more information.
Only approved Builders can post live jobs.
Once approved, the Builder receives a welcome email and gains access to the Builder Console.
 
2. Inspector / Certified Professional Onboarding
An Inspector or Certified Professional creates an account and uploads all required credentials, certifications, registrations, and supporting documentation.
The SiteLine Admin team reviews the submission and either approves, denies, or requests more information.
Only approved Inspectors / Certified Professionals can participate in governed dispatch and receive job assignments.
Once approved, the Inspector receives a welcome email and gains access to the Inspector Console.
 
3. Builder Posts a Job Request
Once inside the Builder Console, the Builder posts an inspection request. This may be for a structural inspection, electrical inspection, or another permit-related inspection type, subject to the platform’s permit-family and credential rules.
The Builder uploads all required project information and supporting documentation, and provides one, two, or three possible date and time options for the inspection.
Before the job can go live, the Builder must pre-fund the full estimated transaction cost into Escrow.
This pre-funded amount includes:
•	the Base Inspection Fee 
•	any applicable Priority Multiplier for rush work 
•	any estimated premium exposure for a potential Hold / Modification Required workflow 
•	the SiteLine 10% platform commission, applied to total transaction volume 
Once the job is fully funded, it is posted to the Live Job Board.
 
4. Governed Dispatch
SiteLine does not operate as an open bid-and-select marketplace.
Instead, SiteLine uses Governed Dispatch.
The platform automatically filters the Live Job Board based on:
•	approved credentials 
•	permit family 
•	discipline 
•	region 
•	stage eligibility 
•	availability 
The first eligible Inspector to claim the posted time slot is automatically awarded the job.
This dispatch model is designed to maximize speed, enforce eligibility, and reduce selection delays for the Builder.
The Builder does not browse, compare, or manually select Inspectors. A Builder may only object to a dispatch through SiteLine Admin, and only for governed reasons such as documented eligibility mismatch, conflict, or material job-context error.
Once the dispatch is claimed, the Builder is notified immediately and the job status changes to Assigned.
 
5. Inspector Travels to Site and Starts Inspection
The Inspector travels to the job site. The platform may provide real-time progress and ETA visibility to the Builder.
Once on site, the Inspector begins the inspection process.
For each permit request, SiteLine provides a standardized stage-specific inspection form. The Inspector responds to each checklist item and uploads:
•	time-stamped photos 
•	geolocated photos 
•	video 
•	field notes 
•	correction evidence, where applicable 
This creates the live evidentiary record while the inspection is in progress.
 
6. Inspection Outcome
While on site, the Inspector provides one of the following outcomes:
•	Pass 
•	Fail 
•	Hold / Modification Required 
The Builder instantly receives a notification of the result.
If the result is Pass
The inspection is complete, the Inspector leaves the site, and the Builder proceeds with construction.
If the result is Hold / Modification Required
If the required correction is minor and can be completed quickly, the Inspector may initiate a Hold / Site-Retainer workflow.
The Builder may then choose to retain the Inspector on site for immediate corrective work, subject to premium hourly rates and platform rules.
If the Builder accepts the Hold:
•	the retainer timer starts 
•	premium hourly charges accrue according to the agreed structure 
•	the Builder performs the correction 
•	the Inspector re-reviews the corrected condition on site 
•	the Inspector uploads additional photos, videos, and notes 
•	the Inspector resolves the result to Pass or Fail 
If the Builder declines the Hold, the inspection proceeds to Fail.
If the result is Fail
The Builder must correct the issue and rebook the inspection at a later date.
 
7. Escrow, Commission, and Payment Release
SiteLine charges a flat 10% commission on total transaction volume.
The 10% take-rate applies to:
•	the Base Inspection Fee 
•	Priority Multipliers, including rush pricing such as x1.5 jobs 
•	premium hourly charges incurred during a Hold / Modification Required workflow 
Payment logic is governed by workflow outcome:
•	If the result is Pass, the escrowed funds are released according to platform payout rules 
•	If the result is Fail, the escrowed funds are released according to fail-payment rules 
•	If the inspection includes a Hold, any additional premium hold charges are calculated, added to the transaction, and then released according to the agreed terms and acknowledgment flow 
SiteLine Admin retains the ability to block payout where a dispute, deficiency, or control-plane exception exists.
 
8. Final Documentation — The Defensible Professional Record
Once an inspection has been completed, the inspection record is bundled into a professional, tamper-resistant, authority-ready package.
This final output is not just a basic certificate. It is a Defensible Professional Record designed for:
•	final authority review 
•	compliance verification 
•	dispute resolution 
•	liability defense 
•	archive retrieval 
The package can include:
•	line-item inspection responses 
•	time-stamped and geolocated photos 
•	videos and field notes 
•	an Evidence Index 
•	correction and hold history 
•	deficiency history 
•	version control 
•	digital seal metadata 
•	professional declarations 
•	required schedules and supporting forms 
•	supporting field review records 
The completed record is then ready for export to the Authority Reviewer / Final Authority or the relevant Certified Professional.
 
9. Authority Access — Controlled Submission Access Only
Authority access must be tightly controlled.
The Authority Reviewer does not receive access to the full SiteLine Vault, internal Admin system, unrelated projects, financial records, or private operational files.
Instead, the Authority Reviewer receives secure, limited, read-only access only to the specific submitted package and its supporting record set for that project and review cycle.
Authority access is:
•	package-scoped 
•	project-scoped 
•	read-only 
•	time-limited 
•	version-controlled 
•	fully audited 
The Authority Reviewer may be able to:
•	view the final package 
•	view the evidence index 
•	view included attachments and supporting material 
•	review correction / hold history included in the submission 
•	acknowledge receipt 
•	return comments, deficiencies, or requests for more information 
The Authority Reviewer may not:
•	browse the full Vault 
•	view unrelated projects or users 
•	view Builder onboarding files 
•	view Inspector credential files 
•	view escrow, payout, or dispute data 
•	modify internal records 
This ensures the authority sees only the exact submission SiteLine intentionally provides for review.
 
10. Authority Bridge
SiteLine supports a controlled Authority Bridge for delivering final packages.
The Authority Bridge may include:
•	secure email delivery 
•	secure read-only review link 
•	portal-assisted submission support 
•	dispatch logging 
•	receipt tracking 
•	deficiency and response tracking 
This allows SiteLine to support real-world authority workflows without requiring every authority to become a full SiteLine platform user.
 
11. The Vault — Archive and Record Retention
After completion, the final inspection record — including pass or fail results, supporting documentation, evidence, and exported package files — is stored in the SiteLine Vault.
The Vault exists to protect Builders, Inspectors, and SiteLine by preserving a searchable, retrievable professional record for compliance, dispute resolution, insurance response, future reference, and property transactions.
The Vault is an internal record-retention and retrieval system. It is not an open browsing system for external authorities.
SiteLine offers tiered archive retention:
Standard Tier (0–2 Years)
Included in the 10% base commission.
This tier provides short-term operational access and immediate record availability after project completion.
Professional Tier (2–10 Years)
Available as a one-time premium fee.
This tier is designed to support professional and commercial risk management, including common defect-discovery and documentation-retention realities.
Legacy Tier (Life of Building)
Available as an ongoing subscription.
This tier supports long-term asset management, future refinancing, resale due diligence, major renovations, and multi-decade property record continuity.
The Vault ensures that completed inspection and permit records remain secure, searchable, and retrievable when needed, while still maintaining strict access boundaries between internal records and authority-facing submissions.
