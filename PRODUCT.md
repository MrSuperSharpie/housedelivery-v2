# Vero: Product & Brand Manifesto

## Core Identity
**Brand Essence:** Clarity you can build on.
**Archetypes:** The Sage (clarity, order, judgment) & The Guardian (protection, accountability).
**Positioning:** Vero is the calm authority in construction compliance. It is the trusted layer between builders, inspectors, owners, and regulators. It is not a contractor directory or a government portal.

## Tone of Voice
* **Clear, not clever:** Plain language. No startup theater.
* **Confident, not arrogant:** Speak with authority, respecting the difficulty of the jobsite.
* **Calm, not cold:** Reduce friction, do not add stress.
* **Premium, not precious:** High-end, but practical for jobsite use.
* **Responsible, not bureaucratic:** Respects regulation while making it navigable.

## Visual Personality (The "Linear + Stripe + Watershed" Hybrid)
Vero must feel like a premium physical object—a legal document or a high-end precision tool.

**Must be:** Quiet, architectural, precise, spacious, professional, premium, highly legible, compliance-oriented. Dark-first, high contrast (WCAG AA).
**Must NOT be:** Playful, generic SaaS (no rounded blue buttons), noisy, cluttered, or "government beige." No gradients, cartoon icons, or excessive animations.

## UI/UX Directives
1.  **Restraint:** Hide what is unnecessary. Trade-specific blinders must remain visually strict.
2.  **Physicality:** The "Master Seal" and approval states should feel final, heavy, and earned.
3.  **Accessibility:** Large, "meaty" touch targets for users wearing gloves on jobsites.
4.  **Typography:** Move away from generic Inter. Use authoritative, heavy typography for headings to ground the interface.

## Vocabulary
* **Use:** Clear, verified, approved, ready, sealed, accountable, compliant, documented, source of truth.
* **Avoid:** Disruptive, revolutionary, frictionless, gamified, hustle, magic, shortcut.
